Request deletion of your NEPSE Pulse account

You can request deletion of your NEPSE Pulse account and associated account data, including cloud watchlist entries linked to your account.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to anishacharya95@gmail.com with the subject NEPSE Pulse account deletion request.
  2. Send the request from the email address associated with your NEPSE Pulse sign-in, if you can. If you cannot access that address, explain this in your message. Do not send your Google password or verification codes.
  3. We may ask for reasonable verification to protect your account. We will confirm when the request has been reviewed and processed, or contact you if more information is needed.

What will be deleted

After we verify and process the request, we will delete your Supabase authentication account and associated cloud watchlist records under our control. Local preferences or cached data stored on your device may need to be removed by clearing the app/browser storage or uninstalling the app. Information that we must retain for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons may be retained only as permitted by law.

How long does it take?

We aim to review requests promptly and complete verified deletion requests within 30 days, unless additional time is reasonably required or retention is legally necessary. We will contact you if there is a delay.

Important: This page provides a way to submit a deletion request; it does not delete an account automatically. The NEPSE Pulse operator must verify the request and perform the deletion in the account system. Do not publish this page until you are prepared to monitor the email address and process requests.
