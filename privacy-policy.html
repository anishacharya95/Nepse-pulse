NEPSE Pulse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

NEPSE Pulse provides Nepal stock-market information and related app features. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app.

Information we handle

When you sign in, our authentication provider (Supabase, with providers such as Google where enabled) may process account identifiers such as your email address, phone number, and basic profile information. If you save a watchlist while signed in, the app may store the symbols associated with your account. Information you choose to submit to the app may also be processed to provide the requested feature.

How information is used

Information is used to sign you in, maintain your session, sync account-linked features such as watchlists, provide app functionality, maintain security, and troubleshoot problems.

Service providers and advertising

The app uses Supabase for authentication and related backend services. The app may display ads through Google AdMob. These providers may process information under their own privacy terms and settings. Please review the relevant providers’ privacy policies for details.

Retention and deletion

You can request account deletion using the Delete Account option in the account screen. Deletion is intended to remove your Supabase authentication account and account-linked data that is configured to be deleted with it. Some records may be retained where required by law, for security, or to resolve disputes. If in-app deletion does not work, contact anishacharya95@gmail.com.

Children and security

Do not submit information that is not needed to use the app. We use third-party services and reasonable safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the app changes. For privacy questions or deletion help, contact anishacharya95@gmail.com.
