Privacy Policy for NEPSE Pulse

Last updated: October 19, 2026

NEPSE Pulse (“we,” “our,” or “the app”) provides tools and information related to Nepal’s stock market. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the NEPSE Pulse website or Android app.

Before publishing: This draft reflects the project files reviewed and its configured Supabase authentication, watchlist table, and planned Android AdMob integration. The operator should verify all data flows and update this policy if the production app uses additional services or collects additional information.

Information we may process

  • Sign-in information: When you sign in with Google through Supabase, the service may process your account identifier and profile details made available by Google, such as your email address, name, and profile image.
  • Watchlist information: If you use cloud watchlists while signed in, NEPSE Pulse stores the stock symbols you save, associated with your account.
  • Device-local information: The website stores some preferences and cached market/interface data in your browser’s local storage. This information may remain on your device until you clear the browser/app data.
  • Technical and advertising information: The Android app integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its advertising SDK may process device identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics, approximate location signals, or other information as described in Google’s policies and your device settings. The precise data depends on SDK configuration and consent choices.
  • Messages you send us: If you contact support, we receive the information you include in your message.

How we use information

Information is used to authenticate users, provide account features and saved watchlists, maintain and troubleshoot the service, respond to support and deletion requests, protect the service, and display or measure advertising in the Android app where enabled.

Service providers and sharing

NEPSE Pulse uses Supabase for authentication and cloud watchlist storage. The Android app integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google sign-in is provided by Google. Market-data, charting, hosting, or other third-party services may receive technical requests when their features are used and process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information in exchange for money. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect users, the service, or legal rights.

Retention and deletion

We retain account and related cloud data while needed to operate the service, provide features, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and enforce our agreements. You can request deletion of your NEPSE Pulse account and associated data using the instructions on our Account Deletion page. Account deletion must be processed by the service operator; sending a request does not mean deletion has already occurred. Some limited information may be retained where legally required or for legitimate security and fraud-prevention purposes.

Children

NEPSE Pulse is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the request.

Security

We use service-provider security features and access controls appropriate to the services we configure, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Never send us your Google password, authentication codes, or private credentials.
